================
Scenarios GL06LP
================

The following scenarios are available by default for the GL06LP model.

Baseline
========

The baseline scenario has the following exogenous values:

- Government demand :math:`G = 20`
- Interest rate on bills :math:`r_b = 0.03`
- Bond price :math:`p_{bl} = 20`

Scenario 1: Rise in Bill Rate
==============================

The interest rate on bills increases from 0.03 to 0.035.

Scenario 2: Rise in G
======================

Government spending increases from 20 to 25.
